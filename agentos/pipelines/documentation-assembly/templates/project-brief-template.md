# Project Brief Template

## Document Control
- Document status: `DRAFT`
- Document purpose: capture the problem, goal, and boundaries before specification work starts
- Source of authority: human-reviewed project sources and scoped task inputs
- Non-approval reminder: this brief does not authorize execution, approval, merge, or release

## Problem
Describe the observable problem. Focus on what is happening now, who is affected, and what concrete failure or friction exists.

Minimum guidance:
- State the problem as an observed condition, not as a proposed solution.
- Name what evidence, report, or direct observation supports the problem statement.

## User or Stakeholder
Name the main user, operator, reviewer, or stakeholder group.

Minimum guidance:
- Explain who experiences the problem.
- Explain who needs the resulting document set.

## Goal
State one verifiable result this brief is trying to make possible.

Minimum guidance:
- Use one concrete outcome.
- Avoid combining multiple independent goals in one sentence.

## Expected Value
Describe why solving the problem matters.

Minimum guidance:
- Explain the practical benefit if the goal is reached.
- Keep value separate from approval or delivery claims.

## Scope
List what is in scope for this brief.

Minimum guidance:
- Name the included work area, document flow, or process boundary.
- Keep scope narrow enough that later documents do not need to invent hidden assumptions.

## Constraints
List the limits that must remain visible downstream.

Minimum guidance:
- Include source authority, repository, branch, approval, safety, and dependency limits when relevant.
- Do not use this section to assign a Risk Profile.

## Non-Goals
List what this brief explicitly does not do.

Minimum guidance:
- Exclude adjacent ideas that may look useful but are not part of the brief.
- Make sure Non-Goals do not contradict Scope.

## Known Risks
List known risks that could affect planning or later execution.

Minimum guidance:
- Describe the risk and why it matters.
- Do not assign or imply a Risk Profile here.

## Unknowns
List unresolved questions or missing facts.

Minimum guidance:
- Keep `UNKNOWN` visible when facts are missing.
- Do not silently convert unknowns into assumptions.

## Success Criteria
List the conditions that would show the brief is good enough for specification work.

Minimum guidance:
- Use reviewable criteria.
- Keep criteria separate from approval.

## Source References
List the exact sources that inform this brief.

Minimum guidance:
- Name files, reports, or human decisions precisely.
- Distinguish direct evidence from assumptions.

## Human Review Boundary
State what a human must still decide.

Minimum guidance:
- Remind the reader that the brief does not authorize execution.
- Identify the next human review or clarification point if one is required.
